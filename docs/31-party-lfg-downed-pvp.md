# Party, LFG, Downed State and PvP

## Party

Default maximum party size is five. Party supports invite, accept, decline, leader transfer, kick, leave, ready check, shared activity and encounter membership.

Role preferences are descriptive, not classes:

- Frontline
- Guard/Control
- Damage
- Support
- Flexible

## LFG

Players list an activity, region, language, role preference, experience note and entry requirements. Automated joining is optional; default flow requests leader acceptance. LFG never exposes hidden Mastery numbers.

## Shared progress

Quest/objective definitions declare whether progress is individual, proximity-shared, contribution-shared or party-instance shared. Item collection cannot duplicate one physical lot to every member unless reward definitions intentionally grant personal copies.

## Downed state

Party PvE only:

- first zero-HP event in an encounter causes DOWNED for 15 seconds unless the attack has Execute;
- ally revive channels 4 seconds and is interrupted by damage/movement;
- revived player returns at 25% HP with a short vulnerability protection that ends on hostile action;
- each character can be revived once per encounter;
- the next zero-HP event is death.

Solo players normally die at zero HP. Content may provide an authored solo revive consumable with strict rules, but none is mandatory.

## Encounter membership

Joining, leaving, disconnecting and rejoining are tracked. Disconnect does not clear threat or duplicate rewards. A short reconnect grace may restore party/instance membership.

## PvP scope

V1 includes consensual duels and instanced arena matches only.

- no open-world flagging;
- no cargo theft PvP;
- no durability loss;
- separate damage/healing/CC profile;
- consumable restrictions declared by arena rules;
- death does not create PvE Death Pouch.

## Duel

Requires mutual acceptance, safe duel region and no active cargo/market/crafting transaction. Duel ends on defeat, surrender, boundary violation or disconnect timeout.

## Arena

Supports small team modes, matchmaking hooks, spectator safety and match snapshots. Arena rewards are bounded cosmetics/renown/currency and cannot be exploited for combat mastery through arranged repeated matches.
