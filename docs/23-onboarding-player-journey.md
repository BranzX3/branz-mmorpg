# Onboarding and First Player Journey

## Goals

The first session must teach the universal control grammar and classless identity without presenting a traditional class selection screen. The player chooses a starting foundation, not a permanent class.

## Login and resource-pack gate

1. Player joins a restricted arrival lobby.
2. Server offers the required versioned resource pack.
3. While pack status is pending, character/world interaction remains locked.
4. Success opens character initialization/onboarding.
5. Decline/failure shows retry, troubleshooting and supported-client guidance.
6. No MMO character session enters the main world without the correct pack SHA.

Enhanced EMF/ETF visuals are detected only for help/presentation messaging and are never required.

## Character initialization

One account creates one persistent character profile automatically from the Minecraft account identity and skin. V1 does not include multiple character slots or a separate appearance editor at creation.

The introduction asks the player to select one starting foundation kit:

- Greatsword
- Sword and Shield
- Bow
- Staff/Ember novice

Crossbow is learned shortly after the introduction because its reload checkpoints add complexity. The choice grants starter knowledge and non-tradeable training equipment. Every other foundation remains learnable later with no permanent penalty.

Starter items:

- cannot be traded or salvaged,
- have low repair cost,
- may be replaced by normal world items,
- do not receive unique endgame traits.

## Tutorial sequence

### Step 1 — Movement and Scene Chronicle

- Explain hotbar slots 1–8 as gameplay slots.
- Introduce Slot 9 Chronicle.
- Open local Scene Hub and identify Equipment, Arts, Journal and Settings.
- Teach that preview does not commit until Confirm.

### Step 2 — Draw and basic chain

- Select starter weapon.
- Observe draw state.
- Perform LMB core chain against a training construct.
- Teach windup/active/recovery through visual/audio cues, not timing numbers.

### Step 3 — Directional action and input buffer

- Perform one directional branch.
- Queue one follow-up during recovery.
- Explain that only one future action is buffered.

### Step 4 — Dodge and guard

- Dodge a slow telegraph using directional Shift.
- Hold stationary Shift to demonstrate crouch/brace distinction.
- Guard a normal hit with RMB where the weapon supports it.
- Perform one generous tutorial perfect guard.

Bow/Staff variants teach their equivalent defense and positioning without forcing a shield interaction.

### Step 5 — F and Q

- Use the equipped F Signature.
- Use the Q Utility.
- Explain that the displayed labels follow the player's swap-hand/drop keybinds.
- Demonstrate that Q does not drop the weapon while Combat Ready.

### Step 6 — Resources and Flask

- Spend stamina/mana.
- Use one Flask charge.
- Show windup/commit/recovery and cancellation before commit.
- Explain Rest Context preparation and boss retry snapshot at a high level.

### Step 7 — Posture and elite encounter

- Fight a tutorial elite with a clear posture bar.
- Break posture and use the prompted finisher/advantage window.
- Elite damage is nonlethal during the first guided attempt, but the tutorial does not grant universal invulnerability outside this encounter.

### Step 8 — Reward and equipment

- Receive a personal reward grant.
- Equip/preview it through Scene.
- Show durability and item identity.
- Introduce the nearest blacksmith, bank and mentor.

## Tutorial adaptation

Each starting foundation maps the same learning goals to its mechanics:

- Greatsword: weapon guard, committed step and posture.
- Sword and Shield: shield guard/perfect guard.
- Bow: draw continuum, positioning and Quiver; guard lesson becomes shove/dodge.
- Staff: mana, interruptible cast and staff defensive utility.

The tutorial records objective completion by semantic capability, not a hard-coded animation or item ID.

## Failure, skip and replay

- First-time tutorial may be skipped only after an explicit warning; essential Chronicle and input setup still runs.
- Returning players may replay tutorial modules from Settings & Help.
- Admin/test accounts may bypass with permission.
- Disconnect resumes at the last safe tutorial checkpoint.
- Definition migration maps active tutorial objective IDs; no silent reset.

## First regional journey

After tutorial:

1. Player enters the starter region with one tracked introduction quest.
2. Quest points to Rest Context, blacksmith, bank, alchemy table and mentor through landmarks rather than exact GPS.
3. First normal combat group validates free combat.
4. Mentor grants a second technique and explains moveset changes at Rest.
5. Regional chain culminates in the first real elite and then the first party-capable boss.
6. Completion unlocks routes to the remaining weapon foundations, including Crossbow.

## Information pacing

Do not show every MMO system at once.

- Session 1: controls, Flask, equipment, durability, Scene.
- Early region: moveset, Rest Context, technique learning, bank and basic crafting.
- First elite: posture, status and remedies.
- First boss: party, checkpoint, personal rewards and pity explanation.
- Later mentor arcs: mastery readiness, forms, attunement and player teaching.

## Acceptance criteria

A fresh tester without external instructions can:

- explain the functions of LMB/RMB/F/Q/Shift;
- distinguish Ready from Engaged behavior;
- open/close Scene without losing items;
- use and cancel Flask correctly;
- identify posture and one defensive response;
- understand that the starter choice is not a permanent class;
- locate the next mentor, Rest Context and blacksmith;
- reconnect mid-tutorial without restarting from the beginning.
