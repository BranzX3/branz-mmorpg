# Lifeskill Overview

## V1 disciplines

- Gathering: Mining, Logging, Foraging, Butchering, Tanning and Excavation familiarity.
- Fishing.
- Hunting.
- Processing.
- Cooking.
- Alchemy.
- Smithing.
- Trading.
- Training (mount handling and caravans).
- Farming.

Every character may learn every discipline. Specialization comes from visible Rank, Mastery, tools, workwear, knowledge, workshops and time investment rather than permanent profession selection.

## Rank

Visible ranks:

```text
Trainee I–V
Skilled I–V
Professional I–V
Artisan I–V
Master I–V
Grandmaster I–V
```

Rank unlocks actions, recipes, tools, mass work, information and contracts. Rank cannot make a veteran produce ten times a beginner's basic output.

## Lifeskill Mastery

Mastery is visible from 0–1000 and combines:

```text
rank contribution
work tool
workwear
lifeskill accessories/charm
regional knowledge
meal/tonic
```

Diminishing-return effects are capped:

- work speed improvement: 35%;
- basic yield improvement: 60%;
- rare-material chance improvement: 30% relative, not absolute;
- batch size/efficiency: discipline-defined caps.

## Life Focus

Life Focus is a soft opportunity resource, not a hard gate.

- Maximum: 100.
- Recovery: 1 per 10 real minutes, including offline.
- Normal work remains available at zero Focus.
- Focused Work spends 1–5 Focus for better yield, rare discovery, speed or rich-node access.
- Focus cannot be bought for real money or traded.

## Evidence and anti-repetition

Progress uses successful committed work plus challenge/variety signals. Repeated low-tier actions remain useful for production but provide reduced rank evidence after the discipline's familiarity threshold. Rich nodes, new regions, contracts, high-tier recipes and high-quality execution provide better evidence.

## Tools and workwear

Tools have Work Speed, Control, Durability, Focus Efficiency and Specialization. Workwear occupies normal gameplay armor slots, provides lifeskill mastery and low combat protection. Changing between workwear and combat gear is allowed out of ENGAGED state; presets require Rest Context for full build changes but individual armor swaps remain ordinary inventory actions with equip delay where configured.

## Economy role

Basic materials remain accessible through common nodes and NPC emergency anchors. Profitable specialization comes from regional knowledge, processing chains, contracts, commissions, rare inputs and logistics rather than monopolizing a mandatory recovery item.
