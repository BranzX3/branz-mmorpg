# Processing and Production Lifeskills

## Processing methods

- Heating
- Chopping
- Grinding
- Drying
- Refining
- Extracting
- Tanning
- Preserving

Processing turns gathered inputs into standardized production materials. It is a visible Lifeskill with rank, mastery, batch size and recipe knowledge.

## Batch work

Players reserve a batch at a station. Early ranks use small batches; higher rank/mastery increases batch size, reduces work time and lowers declared processing loss. There is no unattended infinite queue.

Players may move within a station/workshop radius and use non-conflicting menus while a batch runs. Leaving, combat or station loss pauses/cancels according to recipe policy.

## Cooking

Cooking produces:

- recovery food;
- expedition meals;
- lifeskill meals;
- resistance meals;
- party feasts;
- regional cuisine and trade cargo inputs.

Meal buffs use one category and cannot stack an entire banquet of bonuses. Party feasts create a bounded shared interaction and consume real ingredients.

## Alchemy

Alchemy produces remedies, specialized cures, preventive wards, coatings, Flask preparations, tradeoff elixirs and magical reagents. Rank/mastery affects yield, batch efficiency and recipe access, not random potion rarity.

## Smithing

Smithing produces ingots, components, tools, workwear hardware, repair kits, restoration materials and authored weapon/armor blanks. Artifact and boss equipment may require an NPC master, unique forge or quest component.

## Output attribution

Crafted unique items record crafter character ID/name snapshot, recipe ID, station/city and content version. Commodity lots record origin and production method where relevant to trade or commissions.

## Station economy

Stations consume fuel/fees and durability, creating sinks. City specialties provide recipe access or efficiency, not exclusive control of all basic production.
